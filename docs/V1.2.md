# v1.2 workflows and scientific details

## Numerical controls

Continuous parameters use `step="any"` with physical bounds. The v1.1 minimum
of 0.05 with a step of 0.1 rejected whole ascent/descent rates; burst altitude
and maximum duration had analogous step-base errors. Integer member counts
and seeds remain integers. Backend validation rejects non-positive rates,
non-finite values and invalid altitudes.

## Forecasts

Online choices open the existing download manager with a suggested domain and
time window. Downloads are explicit. Successful forecasts become normal local
datasets; predictions use the pressure-level interpolation engine. No chart
pixels or zonal means are used as atmospheric inputs.

| Product | Source | Available forecast steps |
|---|---|---|
| ECMWF IFS 0.25° | ECMWF Open Data deterministic/control | 00/12 UTC: 0–144 h every 3 h, then 150–360 h every 6 h; 06/18 UTC: through 144 h every 3 h |
| NOAA GFS 0.25° | NOMADS regional subsets | 0–120 h hourly, then 123–384 h every 3 h |

The window is bracketed by real timestamps. GRIB initialization is checked;
conflicting cycles are rejected. GFS auto-selection checks the first/last lead
files and falls back to earlier recent runs. IFS auto-selection uses 00/12 UTC;
explicit 06/18 requests follow the May 2026 operational-stream transition.
Cache signatures identify the model, cycle, fields and regional request.

IFS selected fields are downloaded globally and cropped while decoding. GFS
requests a regional subset using its actual 41-level inventory, not ERA5's
different grid. Atmospheric selections exceeding `max_weather_mb` are rejected
before retrieval; saved size is checked again. Each request spans at most 72 h
and uses a non-dateline-crossing rectangular area.

The reader retains pressure, geometric height, temperature, earth-relative u/v,
humidity and available vertical motion. Pressure vertical velocity (Pa/s) is
converted to m/s. Surface pressure masks below-ground levels. GFS 2 m specific humidity and
ECMWF/ERA5 2 m dew point are retained when supplied; dew point is converted to
specific humidity using surface pressure. Near-surface thermodynamic fields
share the existing 10 m wind anchor. Missing surface humidity is not replaced
with zero. If IFS surface geopotential is unavailable,
the remaining forecast is retained; use fixed landing altitude without terrain.
Actual model-top coverage is checked throughout a flight. IFS levels between
100, 50 and 10 hPa are sparse; high-altitude trajectories depend on interpolation.

Recent forecasts are not a historical archive. Use ERA5/local files for historical
studies. Publication delays, access limits and incomplete runs can still cause
a download or coverage error.

Sources: [ECMWF Open Data](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/forecasts/datasets/open-data),
[ECMWF client](https://github.com/ecmwf/ecmwf-opendata),
[NOAA GFS](https://www.nco.ncep.noaa.gov/pmb/products/gfs/) and
[NOMADS](https://nomads.ncep.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/filter_gfs_0p25.pl).

## Coordinated arrivals

Without atmospheric vertical wind:

`launch UTC = arrival UTC − (target altitude AMSL − launch altitude AMSL) / ascent rate`

Each ascent is then integrated at its own location and UTC times. With vertical
wind, the launch time is iteratively corrected using its simulated arrival
residual, to a 0.25 s tolerance, with up to 12 trials. Nonconvergence or inadequate
weather is reported per balloon. Plans disable sensitivity members; ordinary
flights/series retain them. Up to 80 balloons are allowed.

Evaluate a point at several ascent rates, or use individual rates at saved sites.
The table sorts by launch UTC and shows predicted arrival positions. The dashed
map connector is a formation guide, not another trajectory. Straight-line
alignment is not guaranteed; spatial optimisation of launch sites is not supplied.

Endpoint: `POST /api/predictions/arrival`. Plans retain the series result shape,
with `schedule_type: coordinated`, for JSON/CSV/GeoJSON/KML compatibility. CLI:

```bash
python -m balloon_predictor plan-arrival examples/coordinated-arrival.yml
```

## Sonde NetCDF contract

The shipped `sonde_schema.json` contains definitions only: no original samples,
launch identity, contact information or campaign metadata. Generated files retain
NETCDF4 format; a fixed `time` dimension; variable order/names/dimensions; units;
float64 data; int64 time; NaN float fill values; proleptic Gregorian calendar;
variable-attribute names; and contiguous uncompressed storage. The dimension
length follows the flight, rather than the reference's 1918 samples. Original
global-attribute names/type kinds are retained with simulation-appropriate
values; additional attributes record model provenance and export options.

| Variables | Model-derived meaning |
|---|---|
| `time`, `timePeriod` | UTC coordinate and elapsed seconds at 1 Hz |
| `lat`, `lon`, `alt` | Predicted position, interpolated between integration steps |
| `pressure`, `temp`, `dewp` | hPa, Kelvin temperature, optional Kelvin dew point |
| `relative_humidity`, `specific_humidity` | Liquid-water RH (%) and q (kg/kg), or NaN |
| `u`, `v`, `vel_h`, `windDir` | Winds, horizontal speed and meteorological from-direction |
| `vel_z`, `vel_z_smoothed` | Simulated vertical speed and smoothing |
| `vel_z_prime`, `vel_z_prime_smoothed` | NaN: unresolved observational perturbations/noise |
| `PotTemp` | Dry potential temperature referenced to 1000 hPa |
| `CompRng`, `CompAz`, `heading` | Great-circle range and bearings |

Atmospheric values are sampled at each exported position and UTC time, not in a
fixed column. Pressure-level geopotential heights use the existing spherical
geometric-height conversion. Geoid corrections, sensor lag, radiation effects,
turbulence and instrument noise are not simulated. Metadata for simulated
vertical speed does not claim observed buoyancy/noise separation. Dew point/RH
use liquid-water Magnus approximations, not frost-point or mixed-phase diagnostics.

Preserving int64 seconds and a regular 1 Hz cadence means omitting a fractional
terminal second. The exact event is stored in `exact_endpoint_json`; the map and
arrival table retain that exact target/landing event. Full scope includes descent
when present; ascent-only flights do not gain a fabricated descent.

Download: `GET /api/predictions/{job_id}/sonde.nc?flight_index=0&scope=ascent`
(or `scope=full`). The index selects one flight from a series/plan. Temporary
files are removed once the response has been sent.
